TryCoast 🏄‍♂️

Minimize friction. Maximize flow. TryCoast is on a mission to shrink the gap between your thoughts and your messages, emails, or tasks. Imagine swiping—simple, fluid gestures replacing clicks and keystrokes—powered by generative AI.

🖥️ Desktop & Mobile Experience

  • Caps Lock Binding: Hit Caps Lock to enable the swipe overlay on your laptop. No invasive toolbars—just you and the words flowing.
  • Four-Directional Swipes:
    • Swipe ↑ to pivot your message tone (formal → casual).
    • Swipe → to autocomplete and advance.
    • Swipe ↓ to revert or undo the last AI suggestion.
    • Swipe ← to discard or exit to manual typing.
  • Phone-First: A single-finger gesture transforms your draft—no keyboard contortions needed.

🤖 Under the Hood: Generative AI & RAG

TryCoast leverages ChatGPT-style transformers fine-tuned for short-form communication. We integrate Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) by fetching your recent emails, calendar entries, and notes as context vectors. This ensures that each swipe is informed, accurate, and personalized:

context = retrieve_documents(user_email_history, calendar_events)
prompt  = build_prompt(user_input, context)
output  = ai_model.generate(prompt)

By blending on-device embeddings with cloud-based inference, we strike the balance between privacy and power. Our pipeline supports adaptive prompts, context window sliding, and dynamic token budgeting—pushing state-of-the-art research into your everyday workflow.
